# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective employers, collaborators, and clients evaluating Ade Nafil
Firmansah's experience and fit for software development work.

## Product purpose

This portfolio presents Ade Nafil Firmansah's background, technical experience,
education, and contact details. It gives visitors a concise path to evaluate his
work and reach him.

## Capabilities and constraints

- The site is a statically generated Astro portfolio with light and dark themes.
- Personal and professional claims must come from the portfolio data or CV.
- Organization history is intentionally omitted until verified data is available.
- The site must remain responsive and keyboard accessible.

## Brand commitments

- The public name is Ade Nafil Firmansah, with `adenafil.` as the header mark.
- The custom `N` monogram is used for logo and favicon assets.
- The voice is concise, factual, professional, and approachable.

## Evidence on hand

- Structured profile and experience data in `src/data/portfolio.ts`.
- CV at `public/assets/firmansah.pdf`.
- Portrait at `public/profile.jpg`.
- Logo and favicon assets under `public/`.
- No verified organization history is currently available.

## Product principles

- Make professional information easy to scan.
- Prefer verified facts over decorative claims.
- Keep contact and CV access obvious.
- Preserve a fast, lightweight browsing experience.
