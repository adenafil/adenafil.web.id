## About the project

SIDNOV is a full-stack e-commerce platform developed for an Indonesian brand
that produces handmade bags with contemporary ethnic motifs. It brings the
public storefront, customer purchasing journey, order fulfillment, editorial
content, and store administration into one application.

The customer-facing experience supports product discovery, wishlists, carts,
checkout, account management, order tracking, and reviews. A separate
administration workspace gives store operators control over products, orders,
cashback vouchers, stories, banners, and email broadcasts.

## Product discovery and brand storytelling

Customers can browse the complete catalog or narrow it by bag category. Each
product page combines an image gallery, availability, price, product details,
quantity selection, and direct actions for the cart and wishlist.

## Customer account and checkout

Customers can create an account with email and password or sign in through
Google OAuth. The account area brings together personal details, wishlists,
cashback points, orders, reviews, and cashback vouchers.

service, apply an eligible cashback voucher, or use an available cashback point
balance. Checkout supports Tripay QRIS payments and a manual transfer flow with
payment-proof upload.

details, and record status changes. Customers can inspect their order history,
follow fulfillment progress, and submit reviews after an order is completed.

maintain the catalog and handle day-to-day fulfillment without editing data

## Architecture and integrations

SIDNOV uses Laravel 12 for application logic, routing, authentication, database
access, and integrations. React 19 and TypeScript render the interface through
Inertia.js, while Tailwind CSS provides the responsive presentation layer.
MySQL stores customers, addresses, products, carts, orders, reviews, content,
and campaign data.

Laravel Socialite handles Google OAuth. Tripay creates QRIS transactions and
processes signed payment callbacks, while the checkout service records orders,
line items, rewards, and status history inside database transactions. Shipping
services provide delivery estimates and shipment tracking, and queued email
broadcasts support customer campaigns.
