## About the project

Wordkeep is a personal vocabulary memory system for saving unfamiliar words
without losing the moments in which they appeared. Rather than treating a word
as an isolated flashcard, it connects each term to its original capture, the
source it came from, and every later encounter with it.

The application is intentionally private and single-user. Its workflow starts
with a screenshot or a passage of text, continues through manual curation, and
returns to the same context during review. It is available on desktop and as
an installable mobile web app.

## Capture first, organize later

Capture Sessions group screenshots and text from a particular episode, chapter,
or reading session. The Inbox brings captures from across Sessions into one
curation queue. Multiple screenshots can be uploaded together, and captures
can be moved through inbox, in-progress, curated, and archived states.

When curating a capture, the owner can record a term and its meaning, then
connect it to the part of the source where it appeared. A term can have
multiple encounters, preserving a chronological record of where it was seen
instead of replacing earlier context with a single definition.

## Find words in context

The Terms workspace makes the archive searchable across terms, meanings, notes,
and capture context. Opening a term reveals its encounters in order, with the
associated source media and recognition history. This makes it possible to
revisit a word through the scenes and passages that made it memorable.

## Deliberate review and progress

Review presents a term alongside one of its saved encounters so the owner can
try to recall its meaning before revealing the answer. A review can be marked
unknown, familiar, uncertain, or known, or skipped without a rating. Each term
appears only once in a review session; after a saved review, it normally rests
for 72 hours before returning to the random pool.

words, and changes in recognition. Its activity view can show the past 7 days,
30 days, or year without treating an unrated encounter as a failed review. The
owner can also export the archive's records as JSON.

## An installable mobile workflow

Wordkeep is a responsive PWA with a Web Share Target. On a supported Android
device, screenshots shared from another app can enter Wordkeep's capture flow,
ready to be curated later. The mobile interface keeps Sessions, Inbox, Terms,
Review, and Stats within reach through bottom navigation.

## Architecture and privacy

The frontend is a React and TypeScript application built with Vite, Tailwind
CSS, and an installable PWA shell. A Bun-hosted Hono API manages sessions,
captures, terms, encounters, reviews, statistics, and exports. PostgreSQL and
Drizzle ORM store the archive's structured data, while capture images remain
in private Cloudflare R2 storage and are served through short-lived URLs.

Wordkeep uses server-side session authentication for its single owner. The
service worker caches the static application shell, not private API responses
or capture media. This separation keeps the archive usable as an installed app
without making personal content public or claiming it is available offline.
